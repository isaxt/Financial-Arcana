# Contemporary Divinatory Arts Taxonomy

Structured bilingual dataset on contemporary divinatory arts, tarot symbolism, professional ethics, symbolic interpretation, decision-making under uncertainty and digital trust signals.

This dataset was prepared for the Kaggle profile of Arianne Galfre / Arianne G Voyance as a non-commercial, research-oriented, educational and exploratory resource.

## Files

- `data/divinatory_terms_taxonomy.csv` — 50 bilingual concepts related to divinatory arts, ethics, interpretation, digital trust and structured data.
- `data/tarot_major_arcana_symbolic_taxonomy.csv` — 22 major arcana with symbolic families, decision contexts, emotional contexts and ethical interpretation boundaries.
- `data/ethical_boundaries_divinatory_practice.csv` — 20 professional boundaries and ethical risk areas in contemporary divinatory practice.
- `data/digital_trust_signals_divinatory_practices.csv` — 25 digital trust signals relevant to online divinatory practices.
- `notebook/mapping_contemporary_divinatory_arts.ipynb` — exploratory notebook for Kaggle.
- `docs/data_dictionary.md` — column-by-column data dictionary.
- `docs/KAGGLE_TEXTES_A_COPIER_COLLER.md` — dataset and notebook texts ready to paste into Kaggle.
- `docs/RESPONSIBLE_USE.md` — responsible use note.

## Scope

The dataset does not contain client records, consultation transcripts, personal data, private messages, health data, legal data, financial data or identifiable sensitive information.

It is a conceptual taxonomy designed to structure qualitative knowledge into tabular data for exploration, visualization and discussion.

## Responsible positioning

The purpose of this dataset is not to prove predictive claims. It is intended to document concepts, ethical limits, symbolic systems and digital trust signals associated with contemporary divinatory practices.
