# Responsible use

This dataset is intentionally non-sensitive. It does not include client data, consultation examples, private messages, personal histories, birth dates, contact details, booking data, health data, legal data, financial data or any information that could identify a consultant.

The dataset is a structured conceptual resource. It should be used for educational, exploratory, research-oriented or digital humanities purposes.

## Not intended for

- predicting personal outcomes;
- replacing a human professional consultation;
- diagnosing medical, psychological, legal or financial situations;
- profiling real clients or vulnerable individuals;
- claiming scientific validation of divinatory perceptions.

## Intended for

- exploring symbolic classifications;
- visualizing ethical boundaries;
- comparing trust signals;
- documenting professional terminology;
- building simple educational notebooks;
- supporting transparent discussion around modern divinatory practices.
