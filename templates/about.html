About the Order

{% call window("A brief history of the Order") %}

The Order of the Financial Arcana established its online presence in 2001 and has, for reasons no one can document, remained operational ever since. It holds that every transaction leaves a trace, every market fluctuation carries an omen, and every financial decision contributes to a larger economic destiny.

Its 78 Arcana are a financial reading of the traditional tarot: 22 Major Arcana for the large forces and institutions of economic life, and 56 Minor Arcana in four suits: Wands (ambition and speculation), Cups (desire and consumer behavior), Swords (risk and regulation) and Pentacles (wealth and consumption).

{% endcall %} {% call window("Purpose") %}

We have replaced mysticism with mathematics, but our desire to predict, control and assign meaning to an uncertain future remains largely unchanged. This project uses the parallel between divination and financial prediction as a satirical frame. It does not claim the two are equivalent.

{% endcall %} {% call window("Methodology: three things, kept apart") %}
  • Observed data: rows in the source datasets, processed offline with Python and Pandas.
  • Calculated metrics: statistics computed from those rows (rates, medians, counts), each with its definition and sample size. Values marked DEMO are placeholders.
  • Authored interpretation: card meanings and the Order's commentary. This is fiction. The link between a card and a metric is a symbolic choice, not a finding.

A reading draws cards at random without replacement. The metric shown with each card is frozen at draw time.

  • The transaction datasets are synthetic. They are not real financial histories and may not represent any real population.
  • Fraud and laundering labels are classifications in the datasets, not independent proof of criminal activity.
  • Correlation is not causation. Transaction data cannot establish a person's wealth, debts or intentions.
  • Readings are random entertainment. Nothing here is financial advice, a prediction of your outcomes, or a fraud-detection service.
